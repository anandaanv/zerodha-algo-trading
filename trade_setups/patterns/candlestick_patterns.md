# Candlestick Reversal Patterns

## Purpose
These patterns are used as entry triggers within trade setups (Double Bottom, Double Top, and others). A completed candlestick reversal pattern moves the setup into WATCHING state. The **breakout level** is the price that must be broken on the next candle to trigger entry.

---

## Bullish Reversal Patterns (at lows)

### Hammer
- **Shape**: Small real body at the top, long lower shadow (≥ 2× body), little or no upper shadow.
- **Completed**: Single candle — confirmed when the candle closes.
- **Breakout level**: High of the hammer candle.
- **Meaning**: Buyers rejected lower prices during the candle; strong reversal signal at support.

### Bullish Engulfing
- **Shape**: Two candles. First candle is bearish. Second candle is bullish and its body fully engulfs the first candle's body (opens below first close, closes above first open).
- **Completed**: When the second (bullish) candle closes.
- **Breakout level**: High of the engulfing (second) candle.
- **Meaning**: Buyers overwhelmed sellers completely — momentum shift.

### Piercing Line
- **Shape**: Two candles. First candle is a strong bearish candle. Second candle opens below the first candle's low (gap down) and closes above the midpoint of the first candle's body.
- **Completed**: When the second (bullish) candle closes above the midpoint.
- **Breakout level**: High of the **first (bearish) candle** — since the second candle is piercing through it, breaking its high confirms full reversal.
- **Meaning**: Buyers absorbed the down gap and pushed back above midpoint — strong reversal.

### Morning Star
- **Shape**: Three candles. First is a large bearish candle. Second is a small-bodied candle (gap down, indecision — doji or small real body). Third is a large bullish candle closing into the first candle's body.
- **Completed**: When the third (bullish) candle closes.
- **Breakout level**: High of the third candle.
- **Meaning**: Three-candle transition from bearish to bullish — very reliable.

### Doji at Support
- **Shape**: Open ≈ Close (body is nearly zero). Long shadows in both directions.
- **Completed**: Single candle — confirmed when candle closes.
- **Breakout level**: High of the doji candle.
- **Condition**: Only valid as a reversal signal when it appears at a known support level (second low of a double bottom, neckline retest, etc.). A doji in isolation is not a signal.
- **Meaning**: Complete indecision — neither bulls nor bears in control. At support = sellers exhausted.

### Inverted Hammer
- **Shape**: Small real body at the bottom, long upper shadow (≥ 2× body), little or no lower shadow.
- **Completed**: Single candle — confirmed when candle closes.
- **Breakout level**: High of the inverted hammer candle.
- **Note**: Weaker than a regular hammer. Requires the next candle to be bullish to confirm (which is effectively the breakout candle itself).

---

## Bearish Reversal Patterns (at highs) — mirror of bullish

### Shooting Star
- Mirror of Hammer. Long upper shadow, small body at bottom of candle.
- **Breakout level** (downside): Low of the shooting star candle.

### Bearish Engulfing
- Mirror of Bullish Engulfing. Second candle is bearish and engulfs the first bullish candle.
- **Breakout level** (downside): Low of the engulfing candle.

### Dark Cloud Cover
- Mirror of Piercing Line. Second candle opens above prior high and closes below midpoint of first bullish candle.
- **Breakout level** (downside): Low of the **first (bullish) candle**.

### Evening Star
- Mirror of Morning Star. Three-candle top reversal.
- **Breakout level** (downside): Low of the third candle.

---

## Entry rule (consistent across all patterns)
1. Pattern completes (closing candle confirmed) → setup moves to WATCHING.
2. Next candle breaks the pattern's breakout level.
3. **Entry**: Open of the candle immediately after the breakout candle.

## Notes
- Two-candle and three-candle patterns require all candles to complete before WATCHING is triggered.
- The breakout candle itself is not the entry — entry is the candle after it.
- For patterns at neckline retests (C3), the same breakout logic applies — just the location (neckline zone) is different.
