# Triple Top / Triple Bottom

## What it is
Three consecutive peaks (Triple Top) or troughs (Triple Bottom) at approximately the same price level. Stronger reversal signal than a double top/bottom due to three failed attempts at the level.

## Detection Rules

### Pivot sequence required
- **Triple Bottom**: Low → High → Low → High → Low (5 pivots). All three lows within 4% of each other.
- **Triple Top**: High → Low → High → Low → High (5 pivots). All three highs within 4% of each other.

### Tolerance
- All three peaks/troughs within **4%** of each other.
- Neckline retrace between pivots must be at least **5%**.

### Neckline
- Defined by the highest of the two bounce highs (Triple Bottom) or lowest of bounce lows (Triple Top).

### Target
- Pattern height = neckline − average of three lows (or top equivalent)
- Target = neckline + **1.5×** pattern height (larger measured move than double pattern)

## Status lifecycle
Same as Double Top/Bottom: BUILDING → WATCHING → CONFIRMED

## Confidence scoring (0–95)
- Base: 70 (higher than double — three tests = stronger signal)
- +15 if RSI divergence across the three pivots (progressively improving divergence)
- +10 if MACD divergence

## Elliott wave context
- Triple Bottom often marks end of extended Wave C or Wave 2 at a major degree.
- Triple Top signals exhaustion at end of Wave 5 or B-wave terminus.

## What to watch for (invalidation)
- Any of the three pivots breaking meaningfully below/above the 4% tolerance band.
- Increasing volume on each test of the level (suggests accumulation/distribution, not reversal).
