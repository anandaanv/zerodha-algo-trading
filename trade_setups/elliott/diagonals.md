# Diagonal Patterns (Leading Diagonal / Ending Diagonal)

## What it is
A 5-wave wedge-shaped structure where Wave 4 overlaps Wave 1's price territory. This overlap — which is forbidden in standard impulses — is what defines a diagonal. Diagonals appear at the beginning (leading) or end (ending) of a trend.

---

## Leading Diagonal

### Where it appears
- **Wave 1** of a new impulse (first wave off a major bottom/top).
- **Wave A** at the start of a correction.

### Pivot sequence (bullish)
LOW(p0) → HIGH(p1) → LOW(p2) → HIGH(p3) → LOW(p4) → HIGH(p5)

### Rules
1. **Lows must rise**: p2 > p0, p4 > p2
2. **Highs must rise**: p3 > p1, p5 > p3
3. **Wave 4 MUST overlap Wave 1**: p4 < p1 (W4 low drops below W1 high) by at least **1% of W1 height**
4. **Trendlines converge**: slope of highs (p3−p1) > slope of lows (p4−p2) — the wedge narrows
5. **Wave 2 retraces < 100%**: p2 > p0 (cannot wipe out Wave 1)
6. **Wave 4 retraces < 100%**: p4 > p2 (cannot wipe out Wave 3)

### Confidence scoring (base 50)
- Wave 3 is the longest (W3 > W1 and W3 > W5): +10
- Wave 2 retraces 50–78.6% of Wave 1: +10
- Contracting (W5 < W3 < W1 × 1.618): +10
- RSI divergence (p5 RSI < p3 RSI): +10
- Volume diminishing toward Wave 5: +5
- **Maximum: 90**

---

## Ending Diagonal

### Where it appears
- **Wave 5** of an impulse (terminal exhaustion).
- **Wave C** of a correction (terminal of the correction).

### Pivot sequence (bearish ending diagonal — at top)
HIGH(p0) → LOW(p1) → HIGH(p2) → LOW(p3) → HIGH(p4) → LOW(p5)

### Rules
1. **Highs must decline**: p2 < p0, p4 < p2
2. **Lows must decline**: p3 < p1, p5 < p3
3. **Wave 4 MUST overlap Wave 1**: p4 > p1 (W4 high above W1 low) — the defining criterion
4. **Trendlines converge**: the wedge narrows toward apex
5. **Wave 5 often truncates**: p5 doesn't extend far below Wave 3 low — this is a signal of exhaustion

### Confidence scoring (base 50)
- Wave 3 ≥ 50% of Wave 1 length: +10
- Contracting (W5 < W3 < W1 × 1.5): +10
- **Wave 5 truncation** (p5 > p3, doesn't reach beyond W3): +15 — strong signal of exhaustion
- RSI divergence (p4 RSI < p2 RSI): +10
- **Maximum: 90**

---

## Key distinction from standard impulse
| Feature | Standard Impulse | Diagonal |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Wave 4 overlap W1 | FORBIDDEN (hard rule violation) | REQUIRED |
| Trendlines | Expanding or parallel | Converging (wedge) |
| Wave 3 | Must not be shortest | Typically shortest in ending diagonal |
| Location | Any wave degree | Wave 1, Wave A (leading) or Wave 5, Wave C (ending) |

---

## Trading implication
- **Leading Diagonal at Wave 1**: Deep Wave 2 correction expected after. The diagonal is followed by a sharp counter-trend move before the real impulse begins.
- **Ending Diagonal at Wave 5/C**: Terminal signal — reversal imminent. Price often returns to the origin of the diagonal very quickly (snap-back).
