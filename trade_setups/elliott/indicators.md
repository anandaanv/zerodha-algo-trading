# Indicator Confluence in Elliott Wave Analysis

## Overview
Every ZigZag pivot is enriched with indicator values at that exact bar. These are used to validate wave position (e.g., confirm Wave 3 is the momentum peak, confirm Wave 5 has divergence) and pattern quality.

---

## Indicators computed at each pivot

### RSI (14-period)
- **Wave 3**: Should be the highest RSI in the impulse (peak momentum). Overbought (>70) is positive.
- **Wave 5**: Should show RSI divergence — lower RSI than Wave 3 despite higher price. Signals exhaustion.
- **Wave 4**: RSI should retrace toward neutral (40–60 range).
- **Pattern use**: RSI divergence at pattern bottom (Double Bottom, H&S) = strong reversal signal.

### MACD (12, 26, 9)
- **Histogram amplitude** at each pivot: measured and compared.
- **Wave 3**: MACD histogram should be at its largest (strongest momentum).
- **Wave 4**: MACD amplitude should contract — less than 50% of Wave 3's amplitude.
- **MACD near zero at W4**: If MACD amplitude < 20% of peak — strong W4 signature (+7 pts).
- **Wave 5 divergence**: MACD at W5 peak should be lower than at W3 peak — most reliable sign that W5 is ending.

### EWO — Elliott Wave Oscillator (MACD 5, 35)
- A faster version of MACD specifically tuned to identify Wave 3.
- **Wave 3 EWO peak**: EWO at W3 pivot > EWO at W1 AND > EWO at W5 = classic W3 identification (+10 pts).
- EWO crossing zero is used to identify wave transitions.

### Stochastic (14, 3)
- **Wave 4**: K < 30 (oversold in a bull impulse) = +7 pts. Confirms the Wave 4 correction reached exhaustion.
- Used less for other waves; mainly Wave 4 oversold signal.

### Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 standard deviations)
- **Bollinger Walk** (price > upper band): Indicates Wave 3 momentum. Price sustained above upper band = +7 pts.
- **Bollinger Squeeze** (bandwidth at multi-period low): Breakout imminent. Often precedes Wave 3 or breakout from pattern.
- **%B** (position within bands): Tracked at each pivot.

### ADX (14-period)
- ADX > 25: Trending market — impulse waves more reliable.
- ADX < 25: Ranging market — corrective patterns (triangles, flags, double tops) more reliable.
- Pattern scoring: ADX < 25 at pattern detection gives +5 pts (confirms range environment).

### EMA Stack (20/50/200)
- **BULLISH**: Price > EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200
- **BEARISH**: Price < EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200
- **MIXED**: Any other combination
- Used as a trend filter — a Wave 3 in bullish EMA stack = higher confidence.

### Volume indicators
- **Relative Volume** (vol / 20-period avg):
  - W3 should have expanding volume vs W1: +4 pts
  - W5 should have diminishing volume vs W3: +4 pts
  - Pattern breakout should have expanding volume.
- **OBV (On-Balance Volume)**: Trend of money flow — rising OBV with rising price = confirmation.
- **MFI (14-period Money Flow Index)**: Similar to RSI but volume-weighted.

## Pattern-level indicator use
- **RSI divergence at Double Bottom**: second bottom has higher RSI despite same/lower price = +10 pts
- **MACD divergence at Double Bottom**: same concept with MACD histogram = +10 pts
- **Low ADX for reversal patterns**: ranging market = +5 pts
- **Bollinger squeeze before triangle breakout**: squeeze ending = breakout imminent signal
